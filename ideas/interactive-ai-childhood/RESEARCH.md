# Research notes / Notas de investigación

## Research questions / Preguntas
- Does response-contingent video improve vocabulary comprehension versus passive or pseudo-interactive video? / ¿Mejora la comprensión de vocabulario?
- Do skills transfer to conversations with real people? / ¿Se transfieren habilidades a conversaciones reales?
- What session duration and caregiver participation are appropriate by age? / ¿Qué duración y participación adulta son adecuadas?
- How do voice recognition errors affect different accents and communication needs? / ¿Cómo afectan los errores de voz a distintos acentos y necesidades?
- What privacy-preserving summaries are actually useful to caregivers? / ¿Qué resúmenes respetuosos de la privacidad resultan útiles?

## Background leads to verify / Referencias iniciales por verificar
These are starting points for literature review, **not** a claim that any particular product is currently available or that efficacy has been established. Verify study designs, publication dates and primary sources before citing findings.

## Evidence policy / Política de evidencia
Separate **hypotheses**, **published evidence**, **product claims**, and **original evaluation results**. Do not claim social-skill improvement from engagement metrics alone. / Separar hipótesis, evidencia publicada, afirmaciones comerciales y resultados propios. No equiparar interacción con mejora social.
