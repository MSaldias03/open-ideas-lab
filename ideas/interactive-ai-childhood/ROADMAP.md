# Roadmap / Hoja de ruta

This is a suggested sequence, not a commitment to build. / Secuencia sugerida, no compromiso de desarrollo.

- [ ] **0. Literature and ethics / Literatura y ética:** child-development review, safety threat model, licensing review.
- [ ] **1. Story design / Diseño narrativo:** one original short bilingual story with 2–3 branches and explicit learning objectives.
- [ ] **2. Non-child prototype / Prototipo sin menores:** mock voice input, scripted intent categories, caregiver dashboard with synthetic data.
- [ ] **3. Safety validation / Validación de seguridad:** red-team dialogue, privacy audit, accessibility and accent tests.
- [ ] **4. Expert feedback / Evaluación experta:** educators, speech-language professionals, child-development specialists and caregivers.
- [ ] **5. Ethical pilot / Piloto ético:** only after approvals, appropriate consent, safety safeguards and research design.
- [ ] **6. Evaluation / Evaluación:** learning, transfer, equity, adverse effects, caregiver utility and screen-time impact.
- [ ] **7. Expansion / Expansión:** additional languages, stories and optional licensed-video integration.

## Good first issues / Primeras tareas sugeridas
- Draft a child-safe branching story / Escribir una historia ramificada segura.
- Build a synthetic-data caregiver dashboard / Crear panel parental con datos sintéticos.
- Map privacy and licensing requirements / Mapear requisitos legales y de privacidad.
- Review empirical research and cite primary studies / Revisar investigación con fuentes primarias.
